Automation

Connect the work.

Automation is the layer between your tools. Capture an input once, move it through logic, and deliver an output without repeating the same work manually.

The basic pattern

Think in workflows, not apps.

A useful automation starts with a job. Then identify the trigger, the decisions that need to happen, the actions that follow and the result you want.

01 · Trigger

Something starts the workflow.

A form submission, email, webhook, schedule, payment or database change creates the input.

02 · Logic

Decide what happens next.

Route information, classify it with AI, validate data or apply business rules.

03 · Action

Do the work.

Write to a database, update a CRM, send an email, notify a team or call another service.

04 · Outcome

Deliver something useful.

The workflow should save time, remove repetition, improve consistency or create a measurable result.

Example stack

Form → AI → database → email → follow-up

A visitor submits a form, AI classifies the request, the record is saved and the appropriate follow-up is prepared automatically.

Choose the right approach

Not everything needs automation.

Repeatable work

If the steps are predictable and happen often, automation can remove unnecessary manual effort.

AI-assisted work

Use AI when the workflow needs classification, summarisation, extraction or another judgement layer.

Agentic work

When the system needs to choose between several possible actions, an agent may be appropriate.
