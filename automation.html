Automation

Connect the work.

Automation is the layer between your tools. The goal is simple: capture an input once, move it through a workflow with logic, and deliver an output without repetition.

Recommended stack

Form → AI → database → email → follow-up

Example: a visitor completes a form, AI classifies the request, the record is saved, and a templated email is sent based on the outcome. All without manual work.
