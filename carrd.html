What held up

  • Fastest time-to-live-site of anything we tested — under an hour, start to finish
  • Genuinely responsive on mobile without extra configuration
  • Cheapest paid tier of any tool in this whole stack

Where it struggled

  • One page only — not a fit for anything needing a blog or multiple routes
  • Design flexibility tops out fast once you go past the built-in blocks
  • Free tier keeps Carrd branding in the footer

What we tested

We built three real client pages in Carrd — a portfolio, a product page, and a link-in-bio page — and timed each build against a from-scratch coded page. All three launched in under an hour combined.

Pricing, honestly

Pro starts at $9/year, which is close to a rounding error compared to every other tool in this stack. The free tier is genuinely usable for a single page; you'll want Pro once you need a custom domain.

Pairs well with
