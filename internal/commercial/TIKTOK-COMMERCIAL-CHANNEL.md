# TikTok Commercial Channel

TikTok is now a first-class acquisition channel in the Stack & System strategy.

Role:
- discovery and short-form content
- TikTok affiliate/referral revenue
- traffic into Stack & System where appropriate
- potential promotion of eligible own products
- demonstrations of practical stacks and workflows

Commercial links and programme information remain in the internal commercial layer.
Public website content should focus on useful workflows, tools and outcomes.

Content model:
1. Demonstrate a real problem.
2. Show a useful tool/workflow.
3. Give a concrete takeaway.
4. Use the appropriate TikTok commercial mechanism where eligible.
5. Use Stack & System for deeper guides, systems and products.
6. Apply required commercial disclosure.

The owner supplied a TikTok PartnerLinks URL. No additional programme terms are assumed
without verification.

Production deployment remains paused.
