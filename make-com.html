What held up

  • Visual scenario builder stays readable after a month away from it
  • Largest app library of any tool we tested, including obscure regional apps
  • Built-in error routing (resume / break / ignore) the free tier of most rivals doesn't offer

Where it struggled

  • Operation-based pricing gets expensive fast on high-frequency triggers
  • The module picker is dense — expect a slower first hour
  • Mobile app is read-only; you can't build scenarios from a phone

What we tested

We rebuilt the same three scenarios across Make and two rivals: a Gmail-to-Notion filer (the same one in our free blueprint), a form-to-Slack notifier with conditional routing, and a scheduled RSS-to-newsletter digest. Make was the only one where the branching scenario didn't need a workaround.

Pricing, honestly

The free plan caps you at 1,000 operations a month — enough to trial a real workflow, not to run one daily at volume. Once you're relying on it, budget for the Core plan.
