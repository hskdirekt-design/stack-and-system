Affiliate marketing

Recommend software that solves a problem.

Affiliate marketing works best when the recommendation is part of something genuinely useful. Instead of simply listing a product, show the visitor what the tool does, who it is useful for and where it fits into a workflow.

Affiliate content

Comparison content

A useful comparison shouldn't simply declare one tool "better". It should explain the differences that actually matter to someone trying to solve a particular problem.

Compare features, workflows, ease of use, integrations, pricing structure and the type of user each option suits.

Affiliate content

Tutorials

Tutorials are valuable because they demonstrate the tool in context. A visitor can follow the process and understand why the tool is useful before deciding whether to try it.

The strongest tutorials begin with a clear outcome rather than simply walking through every feature.

Affiliate content

Workflow examples

A workflow example shows where a tool sits inside a bigger system. Instead of recommending software in isolation, you demonstrate how it connects to other tools.

This is especially useful for AI and automation because the value often comes from the connection between several services rather than from one tool alone.

Digital products

Step-by-step guides

A good guide takes someone from a defined starting point to a defined result. It should remove uncertainty, explain the important decisions and give the reader a practical sequence to follow.

The more specific the outcome, the easier it is to create a product that people understand and value.

Digital products

Templates & frameworks

Templates make a product useful because the customer doesn't have to start from a blank page. They provide structure while leaving enough flexibility for the buyer to adapt the system to their situation.

Examples include content frameworks, business checklists, prompts, workflow structures and planning templates.

Digital products

Automation packs

An automation pack can package the thinking behind a workflow into something another person can implement.

It could contain the workflow structure, prompts, setup instructions, data fields, conditions and documentation needed to reproduce the process.

Agent kits

An agent kit packages the instructions and supporting components needed to make an AI agent useful for a specific task.

The important part is the job the agent performs. A useful kit should make the purpose, inputs, tools and expected output clear.
