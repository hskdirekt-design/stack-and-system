Transparent guidelines covering our data privacy, tracking standards, and affiliate marketing commercial model.

Regulatory Statement

This framework is designed to align explicitly with the UK CAP Code (Section 2) on advertising visibility, the United States FTC Endorsement Guides, and standard GDPR/UK Data Protection Act data rules.

1. Affiliate & Commercial Disclosure

Stack & System operates as an independent review hub and pipeline builder. To maintain our testing infrastructure, some of the software platforms linked across our tool directories, blueprints, and review entries contain tracked referral tracking parameters.

If you execute a registration or sign up for a commercial subscription tier via one of these paths, we may earn a referral attribution credit. Please note:

  • This introduces zero auxiliary expense to you (tariffs remain standard or discounted).
  • We completely separate engineering logic from commercial incentives. If a tool fails our operational criteria, it will receive a "Mixed" or poor rating regardless of partnership terms.

2. Data Collection & GDPR Protocols

When interacting with our ecosystem (such as registering for The Signal newsletter or submitting a form setup via our active integration layers), we handle your data with tight protection protocols.

2.1 Email Infrastructure

Your address is solely utilized to transmit system blueprints, architectural reports, and strategy dispatches. We do not engage in list brokerage, data leakage, or external tracking profiling. You maintain a permanent, zero-friction right to unsubscribe via the link embedded in any dispatch footer.

2.2 Active Cookies & Telemetry

We leverage highly minimized, privacy-focused analytical cookies to log standard traffic distributions and behavioral metrics. IP tracking arrays are permanently masked at the ingestion tier.

3. Technical System Indemnity

While the step-by-step documentation published within our operational Blueprints (e.g., our data pipelines connecting external APIs) is engineered defensively and verified line-by-line, execution remains at your own operational risk. Stack & System accepts zero liability for system rate limit flags, API logic failures, schema drift errors, or database overwrites occurring inside your private workspace environments.
