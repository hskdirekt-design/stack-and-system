The Signal

Useful updates. No noise.

The Signal tracks useful AI launches, automation opportunities, emerging tools and online-business trends worth paying attention to.

The idea

What's changing — and why should you care?

The internet produces more information every day. New AI tools launch, existing platforms change, automation becomes easier and new business opportunities appear.

The Signal is designed to filter that information down to developments that could actually matter to someone building with AI, automation and online systems.

The focus isn't simply on what's new. It's on whether a development creates a useful opportunity, changes an existing workflow or is worth testing.

AI signal

AI & emerging technology

AI capabilities are changing quickly. The useful question isn't simply which model is newest. It's what the new capability makes possible.

A new capability can make an existing workflow faster, make a previously expensive process accessible or create an entirely new product opportunity.

What makes an AI signal useful?

  • A meaningful improvement in capability.
  • A new way to perform a useful task.
  • A significant change in cost or accessibility.
  • A new opportunity for automation or product creation.

Tool signal

New tools & launches

New tools are appearing constantly. The Signal focuses on products that have a practical reason to exist rather than simply listing every launch.

A useful tool should have a clear job, solve a meaningful problem or make an existing workflow significantly easier.

Questions worth asking

  • What problem does the tool solve?
  • Who is it actually useful for?
  • What does it replace or improve?
  • Can it connect to an existing system?
  • Is it worth testing now?

Automation signal

Automation opportunities

Automation opportunities often appear when a previously manual process becomes possible to connect, classify or execute automatically.

The opportunity might be small — removing one repetitive task — or large enough to become an entire workflow or product.

Look for repetitive work

Repeated data entry, copying information between tools, sorting enquiries, generating routine reports and sending predictable follow-ups are all potential starting points.

Business signal

Online-business opportunities

Technology creates opportunities when it changes what one person or a small team can produce, distribute or automate.

A useful opportunity might become an affiliate content strategy, digital product, automation service, software product or implementation package.

Start with the problem

Instead of asking "How can I make money from this new technology?", ask "What problem has become easier to solve because of this technology?"
