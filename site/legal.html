Affiliate Disclosure

Stack & System (hskdirect.co.uk) participates in affiliate marketing programmes, including but not limited to Make.com, ElevenLabs, Tally.so, and Carrd. This means that when you click certain links on this site and go on to sign up or purchase, Stack & System may earn a commission — at no additional cost to you.

This complies with the following standards, which govern affiliate marketing for a UK-based, US-audience-reachable site:

  • FTC 16 CFR Part 255 (US): material connections between endorsers and brands must be clearly and conspicuously disclosed.
  • UK CAP Code & ASA guidance: marketing communications must be clearly identifiable as such, and affiliate relationships must not be disguised as independent editorial opinion.
  • UK Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations 2008: commercial intent must not be concealed from consumers.

In practice, this means:

  • Every outbound affiliate link on this site is marked with rel="sponsored" for search engines, per Google's own guidance on paid/affiliate links.
  • Tool recommendations reflect direct, hands-on use by the site operator — not the size of the commission.
  • This disclosure is linked from the footer of every page, and referenced inline near affiliate content.

Only recommend a tool you have actually used and would use again. Regulators and platforms (Make.com, ElevenLabs, etc.) can and do suspend affiliate accounts for misleading claims, undisclosed links, or incentivised reviews — keeping this page accurate protects both the audience and the affiliate relationships themselves.

Privacy Policy

What this site collects

  • Analytics data (e.g. pages visited, approximate location, device type) if an analytics tool is installed.
  • Form submissions — email address and any details submitted via Tally.so forms or the blueprint download flow.
  • Affiliate click data — outbound link clicks may be tracked by the third-party affiliate networks themselves (Make.com, ElevenLabs, Tally, Carrd), not by this site directly.

Legal basis (UK GDPR / Data Protection Act 2018)

Data is processed on the basis of consent (for marketing emails and non-essential cookies) and legitimate interest (for essential site functionality and fraud prevention).

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Requests can be sent via the contact route on the Instagram profile.

Third parties

This site links out to third-party services (Make.com, ElevenLabs, Tally.so, Carrd, and any digital-product checkout provider such as Gumroad or Payhip). Each operates under its own privacy policy once you leave hskdirect.co.uk — this policy only covers data collected directly on this domain.

Data retention

Form submissions and email addresses are retained only as long as needed to deliver the requested content (e.g. the free blueprint) or fulfil a purchase, plus any period required by UK tax law for sales records.

Cookies

This site may use essential cookies (to remember basic preferences) and, if analytics or affiliate tracking is added, non-essential cookies for that purpose. Under the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), non-essential cookies require your consent before being set — if a cookie banner is added, it must offer an equally easy "reject" option alongside "accept."

Digital Products & Refunds

Blueprint packs and templates sold on this site are digital goods delivered instantly on purchase. Under UK Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, the standard 14-day cooling-off right for online purchases does not apply once a digital download has begun with the buyer's explicit consent and acknowledgement that this waives the right to cancel — this consent should be captured at checkout (most checkout providers, including Gumroad and Payhip, do this automatically).

Recommended policy: no refunds once a file has been downloaded, except where the product is faulty, not as described, or as otherwise required by law.
