Think backwards

Define the outcome before choosing the technology.

The outcome is the result you actually want.

For example, instead of saying: "I need an AI agent," you might say: "I want customer enquiries answered quickly, with difficult cases sent to a human."

That gives us something meaningful to design around.

Build the solution

The solution is bigger than the tool.

Once the problem and desired outcome are clear, we can work backwards to design the simplest practical solution.

  1. Define the outcome.
  2. Map the current process.
  3. Identify unnecessary manual work.
  4. Decide what can be handled with normal software.
  5. Identify where AI adds useful capability.
  6. Decide whether an AI agent is actually necessary.
  7. Connect the components with automation.
  8. Keep human approval where appropriate.
  9. Measure whether the solution actually improves the outcome.

The principle

Don't start with the tool.

Tools change. AI models change. Automation platforms change. Your underlying problem and desired outcome are usually much more important.

Once you understand the outcome, you can evaluate the technology that is actually appropriate for the job.
