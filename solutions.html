Think backwards

Define the outcome before choosing the technology.

The outcome is the result you actually want.

Instead of saying: "I need an AI agent," you might say: "I want customer enquiries answered quickly, with difficult cases sent to a human."

That gives us something meaningful to design around.

The Stack & System principle

Don't ask "What AI tool should I buy?"

Ask: "What am I trying to achieve, and what is the simplest reliable system that can achieve it?"

That change in thinking prevents unnecessary software purchases and makes it much easier to understand where AI, automation and agents actually belong.
