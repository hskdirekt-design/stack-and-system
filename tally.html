What held up

  • Free plan has genuinely no response cap — rare in this category
  • Editing feels like typing in a Notion doc, not configuring a form builder
  • Native Make.com integration means zero setup friction downstream

Where it struggled

  • Conditional logic jumps are basic compared to dedicated survey tools
  • Payment field collection requires the paid plan
  • Support replies took longer than Make's or ElevenLabs'

What we tested

We built a client intake form with a conditional question, a file upload, and a payment field. Tally matched every paid competitor we tried except on the payment field, which is gated behind its Pro plan.

Pricing, honestly

The free plan has no response limit — unusual and genuinely useful for a solo operator collecting leads. You only hit a paywall for payments, branding removal, or advanced logic.
