How the directory works

Editorial data, not a marketplace copy.

Stack & System maintains its own editorial information about the companies and technologies included here.

A company can be included because it is useful without Stack & System having an affiliate relationship with that company.

Affiliate relationships and other commercial connections are recorded separately.

This means the directory can grow beyond any single technology marketplace while remaining useful to people building AI-powered businesses and systems.
