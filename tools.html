Our approach

We don't just list tools.

A software directory is useful when it helps you understand what a product actually does and whether it belongs in the system you're trying to build.

That's why Stack & System separates technology discovery from commercial relationships.

A company can be included because it is useful. An affiliate relationship is recorded separately and only when it has actually been established.

Editorial data

Independent from any marketplace.

Partner programmes can help us discover companies worth researching, but Stack & System's directory is not dependent on any single marketplace.

That means a useful company can remain part of the directory even if its partnership programme changes, and excellent technology outside PartnerStack can also be included.

Commercial relationships are recorded separately from our editorial decision to include a technology.
